Fresh Field, enhanced with ideas from your examples

Both versions keep what already works (white cards, dark text, three plain colours, the map and the sorted bars) and add what your examples do well: a row of icon cards for the main numbers, clearer filters, maize artwork that carries the theme, and a simple table for people who prefer rows to charts.

Version 1 · like the crop dashboard

Green Sidebar

Filters and page links move into a green sidebar on the left, with a maize plant growing at its foot. Four icon cards across the top, then the map, the bars, and a "markets to watch" table with the spotlight.

Version 2 · like the Starbucks dashboard

Harvest Banner

The green header becomes a banner with a large maize illustration, the way the Starbucks page leads with its cup. Icon cards sit just under the banner. The right column adds "other foods this month", each food with its own small icon.

Real data: WFP via HDX HAPI, retail white maize, September 2026. All artwork and icons are drawn for this project. In Power BI the background, sidebar and artwork become the page image; the numbers, map, bars, table and dropdowns are live visuals.
