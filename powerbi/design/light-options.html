Light designs: easy for everyone to read

Same page and same real numbers (retail white maize, September 2026), now with dark text on light backgrounds. Colours are kept to three plain meanings: red = too expensive, grey = normal, blue = too cheap. A bigger dot or a longer bar means a bigger change.

Option A · yellow and white

Harvest Cream

Warm maize-cream page with a gold line across the top and two maize cobs lying in the header. White cards with a soft shadow and a small gold marker beside each title.

Option B · green

Fresh Field

A deep green header band with light leaf curves and a gold edge, over a pale green page. White cards keep every number easy to read; the green says "agriculture" at a glance.

Option C · white, maize and a little green

Clean Harvest

Almost all white, like a printed report. Maize shows up as the gold line under the header and the cob logo; green appears only in the tabs and a thin wave of grass along the bottom.

The content is drawn here to match the Power BI visuals; in the report the cards, map and bars are live Power BI visuals on top of the chosen background image.
