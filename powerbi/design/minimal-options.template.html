Green Stalk, minimalist versions

Same layout as the Power BI page, same real numbers (retail white maize, September 2026), four calmer backgrounds. The maize identity stays in small touches: the cob logo, gold accents, and one quiet detail per design.

Option 1

Single Leaf

Flat deep green and near-solid panels. One long maize leaf with a thin gold vein sweeps across the page, seen faintly through the panels. Calm, but still clearly about maize.

Option 2

Gold Line

The most minimal: near-black green, outlined panels, and gold hairlines under the header and along the bottom. Page tabs become simple text with a gold underline.

Option 3

Kernel Corner

Solid rounded panels on deep green, with a faint pattern of maize kernels fading out from the top-right corner. A small piece of texture, nothing more.

Option 4

Evening Glow

A soft green gradient with one warm gold glow in the corner, like late sun over a field. Light glass panels and underlined tabs. Premium without any pictures.

The content on each page is drawn here to match the Power BI visuals; in the report itself the cards, map and bars are live Power BI visuals on top of the chosen background image.
