Maize theme: four dashboard designs

Each option is the overview page of the Cameroon Food Price Watch report, drawn at Power BI's 16:9 size with real WFP data for September 2026. Hover over dots, lines and kernels to see the numbers.

All four share one alert language, checked for colour blindness: warm = too expensive (buyers hurt), blue = too cheap (farmers hurt), grey = normal. A solid mark is an alert (40%+ from normal), a hollow ring is a watch (20–40%). Maize gold is used only for decoration and headlines, never for alerts.

Option A · dark

Harvest Night

Dried-maize gold on a warm charcoal, like a cob under a market lamp. Builds on your Solar layout (side menu, KPI strip, spotlight panel) but with a maize identity and a map at the centre.

Option B · light

Husk Paper

Editorial, like a newspaper front page: a green husk band, cream paper, and a one-sentence headline that says the finding in plain words. The main chart shows every market's price against its normal, so the gap is the story.

Option C · dark

Green Field

The most playful: a deep field green with a faint kernel-dot texture and a bento grid. Its signature chart is the cob, where every kernel is one month of Maroua's maize price, so five years read like an ear of corn.

Option D · light

Market Day

Warm sand, terracotta and indigo, like cloth at a market stall. It leads with the farming calendar (lean season and harvest) behind the national price line, then splits the story into buyers and farmers side by side.

Data: World Food Programme via HDX HAPI (CC BY-IGO), retail white maize, September 2026. "Normal" is the median for the same market and month over the previous five years; markets with under three years of history are shown as hollow dashed circles. Forecast for October 2026 from the project's model. Design previews only: the final report is built in Power BI.
