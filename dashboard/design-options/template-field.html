Maize field designs: round two

Four new overview pages where the maize plant is part of the artwork: stalks, leaves, tassels and cobs, drawn as illustrations so they belong to you and export cleanly as Power BI background images. Green leads, with maize yellow and white.

The alert language stays the same as before, checked for colour blindness: red = too expensive, blue = too cheap, grey = normal; solid = alert, hollow ring = watch. Hover any chart to see the numbers.

Option E · light

Dawn Field

A maize field at sunrise fills the whole page: soft yellow sky, rows of green plants with ripening cobs, the sun behind the title. The data floats on frosted white glass above the crop.

Option F · light

Golden Cob

A giant cob runs down the left edge, its green husk peeling open to become the menu. The headline numbers sit inside big yellow kernels. Clean white page, green type.

Option G · dark

Green Stalk

Night-green with huge maize leaves sweeping across the page and golden tassels at the top. Dark glass cards. The signature chart shows next month's likely price range for every market against its normal.

Option H · light

Kernel Mosaic

The calmest: white page, a fresh green leaf band on top, and a huge cob watermark made of pale yellow kernels behind the charts. Reads like a polished official report.

Data: World Food Programme via HDX HAPI (CC BY-IGO), retail white maize, September 2026. "Normal" is the median for the same market and month over the previous five years. Forecast for October 2026 from the project's model. All maize artwork is drawn in code for this project. Design previews only: the final report is built in Power BI.
